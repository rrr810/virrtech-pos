      About

      VirrTech Duka POS v0.1.0 — a prototype point of sale for small Kenyan shops. All data lives in this browser (IndexedDB); payments are simulated and no real money moves.

      This prototype is not KRA eTIMS certified and must not be used for real tax reporting. M-Pesa and card steps are on-screen simulations — no provider APIs or credentials are used. Currency is the Kenyan Shilling (KSh), locale en-KE, demo location Eldoret, Kenya.
